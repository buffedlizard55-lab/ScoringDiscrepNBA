# Case DISC-20241023-GSW-POR-001 - Warriors vs Trail Blazers Scoring Error

**Date:** 2024-10-23
**Game:** Golden State Warriors @ Portland Trail Blazers, Moda Center
**Final Original:** GSW 139 - POR 104 (243 total)
**Final Corrected:** GSW 140 - POR 104 (244 total)
**Discrepancy:** 1 point, total 243 vs 244 - matches 213/214 pattern

## Incident Summary
With 2:00 remaining in Q3, De'Anthony Melton attempted 2 free throws. He made 1 of 2, but statisticians recorded 0 of 2. The error was not caught live.

## What Changed
- Melton: 10 pts -> 11 pts
- GSW: 139 -> 140
- Total: 243 -> 244
- Melton FT: 0/2 -> 1/2 in that sequence

## Cause
Human error by in-arena statistician. Confirmed by NBA.

> "Statisticians at the game recorded Melton as having missed both free throws in that sequence, even though Melton made the first one." - ESPN
