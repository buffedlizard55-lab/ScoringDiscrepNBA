HOW TO READ THIS SITE

Different numbers
need different explanations.

A live scoreboard mismatch, an NBA correction, and a stale recap are not the same event. We label the source and the confidence before drawing a conclusion.

01

Preserve the observation

Keep the initial score, the source that showed it, and the time precision. Never replace old values with the current value.

02

Compare source roles

NBA records and announcements are primary for official status. A secondary feed can be delayed or internally inconsistent; a mismatch alone is not proof.

03

Show what is unknown

Missing timestamps, event-level scores, and causes stay marked unknown. Calculated values are labeled and their arithmetic is shown.

04

Earn the statistics

Rates and typical durations require full coverage and reliable timestamps. Until then, the dashboard shows sample counts and says what cannot be measured.

§

Official scoring reference
NBA Rule No. 5 defines scoring and says the running score is official if a score discrepancy cannot be resolved.
