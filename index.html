03 / HOW TO READ THIS

Evidence over inference.

The record separates what the league corrected from what another scoreboard or story happened to display.

A

Preserve both states

Original, corrected, and current final values sit side by side. A later value does not erase the earlier report.

B

Trace every claim

Open the evidence detail on any case for claim-level citations, a time-aware timeline, source conflicts, and limitations.

C

Keep unknowns visible

“Derived,” “unverified,” and “not independently captured” mean exactly that. No population rate is shown without a defensible denominator.

i

Coverage note. The confirmed records are a small, non-exhaustive research seed. Live monitoring begins with the first successful scheduled run; it cannot reconstruct earlier source versions or detect every change between polls.
