Evidence standard

NBA CDN scoreboard is an NBA-published feed, not the official scorer's signed record. ESPN is a secondary feed. An automated mismatch is a candidate, not proof that either source—or the NBA official record—was wrong. No historical frequency or 213/214 rarity estimate is available without a verified corpus and denominator.
